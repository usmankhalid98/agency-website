Built the full brand from the ground up for this modest abaya label. Logo, visual identity, and a clean e-commerce site that reflects the elegance and simplicity of their garments.

The Brief

Khaalis needed a brand that felt as refined and intentional as their garments. A modest abaya label with no existing visual identity, they came to us looking for everything from scratch.

The challenge was creating a brand that communicated elegance and simplicity without relying on loud visuals or trendy aesthetics. The identity needed to feel timeless, respectful of the brand's values, and versatile enough to work across packaging, digital, and print.

Approach

We started with deep research into the modest fashion space, understanding the audience, competitors, and cultural context. Every decision was deliberate.

The logo was designed to be clean and minimal, a wordmark that carries quiet confidence. The colour palette draws from natural, muted tones that reflect the brand's ethos of understated beauty. Typography was selected for legibility and elegance across both digital and physical touchpoints.
