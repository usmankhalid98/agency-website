Website Design

Teal Optical

Designed and developed a modern website for an independent high-street opticians. Making it easy for customers to browse services, book eye tests, and find the practice.

Client Teal Optical
Services Website Design + Development
Year 2025

The Brief

Teal Optical is an independent high-street opticians that needed a digital presence to match the quality of their in-store experience.

Their existing online presence wasn't doing them justice. Customers couldn't easily find information about services, book appointments, or get a feel for the practice. They needed a website that was welcoming, professional, and functional, one that would bring new patients through the door.

Approach

We focused on clarity and ease of use. Every page was designed around the key actions: finding the practice, understanding services, and booking an eye test.

The design is clean and modern, with clear hierarchy and generous whitespace that makes information easy to scan. We chose a colour palette that feels clinical yet warm, giving the site a trustworthy, approachable tone that mirrors the in-store experience.
