The Challenge

Wokology is a noodle takeout concept that needed a visual identity to evoke East Asian street-food culture without tying the brand to a single cuisine or language. Using Chinese, Japanese, or Korean characters could unintentionally narrow how the menu or brand is perceived.

The challenge was to create a mark that feels culturally evocative and authentic without being restricted to one specific regional cuisine. The logo needed to use English letterforms while still capturing the energy, texture, and movement of East Asian visual traditions.

Our Approach

The typographic style references the expressive qualities of East Asian brush lettering and street signage — capturing the energy, texture, and movement of those visual traditions while keeping the name readable for a broad audience.

Rather than illustrative elements or cultural iconography that could pigeonhole the brand, we focused entirely on the letterforms themselves. The result is a mark that communicates the spirit of East Asian noodle culture while giving the brand the flexibility to represent a wider range of noodle traditions.
