Career Development Academy

Developed a practical, professional brand identity for this education and training company preparing professionals for careers in business analysis. Brand guidelines and ongoing social media content.

Client Career Development Academy
Industry Education & Training
Services Brand Identity, Guidelines, Social Media
Year 2025

The Challenge

Career Development Academy (CDA) is an education and training company focused on preparing professionals for careers in business analysis. They needed a brand that felt credible and professional while remaining accessible to a growing learning community.

The challenge was creating a visual system that could scale across digital platforms and multiple touchpoints — from the website to social media to course materials — while maintaining a clear, consistent voice that spoke to both aspiring and established professionals.

Our Approach

We developed the full brand identity and produced comprehensive brand guidelines to establish a clear and consistent visual direction across all touchpoints. Every element was built with scale in mind — the system needed to work as hard on a social post as it did on a course certificate.

Beyond the identity, we managed and developed social media content to build awareness, maintain visual consistency, and support ongoing engagement with prospective students. The brand system was built to scale across digital platforms while remaining accessible to a growing learning community.

What We Delivered

01

Brand Identity

Full visual identity system including logo, typography, colour palette, and imagery direction tailored for the education sector.

02

Brand Guidelines

Comprehensive guidelines documenting brand usage, tone of voice, and application rules across digital and print touchpoints.

03

Social Media

Ongoing content management and creation to build awareness, maintain visual consistency, and engage prospective students.
