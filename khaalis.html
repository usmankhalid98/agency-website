Khaalis

Built the full brand from the ground up for this modest abaya label. Logo, visual identity, and a clean e-commerce site that reflects the elegance and simplicity of their garments.

Services Logo, Brand Identity, Web Design

The Challenge

Khaalis is a modest abaya label with a clear vision but no visual identity. They needed everything built from scratch — a logo, a complete brand system, and an e-commerce website that could communicate the elegance and simplicity at the heart of their garments.

The challenge was creating a brand that felt premium and timeless without relying on loud visuals or fleeting trends. It had to resonate with their audience, respect the brand's values, and work seamlessly across digital, print, and packaging.

Our Approach

We began with deep research into the modest fashion space — understanding the audience, the competition, and the cultural context behind the brand. Every decision from colour to typography was intentional.

The logo was designed as a clean wordmark that carries quiet confidence. The colour palette draws from natural, muted tones reflecting understated beauty. Typography was chosen for elegance across both digital and physical touchpoints. The e-commerce site was built to feel as refined as the garments themselves — minimal, intuitive, and focused on the product.
